### 🛍️ What I Do — eCommerce Side

I help Shopify store owners fix what's costing them sales and scale what's working. My work spans:

- **Store Design & UI/UX** — redesigning stores for clarity, trust, and conversions
- **Full Store Audits** — finding the exact points where visitors drop off before buying
- **Email Marketing** — flows and campaigns that recover carts and drive repeat purchases
- **Ads Strategy** — Meta, Google & TikTok ad campaigns built around real store data
- **Conversion Rate Optimization** — turning traffic into paying customers

**My approach:** I lead with value. Before a client commits, I often build a free redesign of their store to show exactly what's possible — no guesswork, just proof.

### 🤝 What I Do — Offline Side

I build and lead a team at **Neolife**, running a physical office and focused on developing people into leaders — not just growing headcount. My work there includes training, systems, and team leadership.
